About the Mission

Our Vision

CrimeTrack is India's premier digital infrastructure for citizen-centric law enforcement. Developed by the National Informatics Center, our mission is to provide a transparent, efficient, and accessible platform for the citizens of India to interact with law enforcement agencies.

Since our inception in 2024, we have processed over 1.2 million verified reports, bridging the gap between community intelligence and tactical police action. We leverage 256-bit AES encryption to ensure that every piece of intelligence shared remains classified and secure.

Safety First

Your safety is our priority. We work 24/7 to monitor and respond to incidents reported through our portal.

Innovation

Built with state-of-the-art technology to ensure real-time updates and zero latency in emergency reporting.
