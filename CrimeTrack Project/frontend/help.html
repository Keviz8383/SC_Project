Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I track my complaint status?

Navigate to the 'Track Status' page from the main header and enter your unique Case ID (e.g., CT-XXXX) to see real-time updates.

2. Is my identity kept confidential?

Yes, all reports are encrypted. Personal data is only accessible to authorized investigators assigned to your case.

3. Can I report a crime without an account?

For security and authenticity, a verified account is required to file official reports. You can quickly sign up on the home page.
